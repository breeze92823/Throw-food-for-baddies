# Throw Food for Baddies

## Repos
- Frontend (this repo): `C:\ThreeJS\Throw-food-for-baddies\Throw-food-for-baddies`
- Backend: `C:\ThreeJS\Throw-food-for-baddies\Throw-food-for-baddies-backend` (Colyseus + MongoDB). The frontend only uses it for **bases** so far (`src/net.js`): progress is still saved to localStorage (`game/state.js`) and the leaderboards are offline stand-ins (`BOARD_BOTS`).
- **Bases / room size**: the hub has `PENS.xs.length` (5) bases and a room holds exactly that many players (backend `BASE_COUNT` = `ROOM_MAX_CLIENTS` = the `seatCap` sent to Bloxity on deploy; keep all in step). At boot `main.js` joins the room (`net.connect()`, through the Bloxity matchmaker as the hosting docs require: `Legion.SDK.net.resolveEndpoint`, a new Client per resolve, retry with backoff up to ~60 s) and the server assigns this player a base index (`PlayerState.base`). `myPen = pens[base]`, `setSpawn()` (player.js) puts the player in front of it, the other bases show who sits there (`Pen.setOccupant`: name + picture) or "Free base". A dropped room is rejoined asking for the same base; a different one reloads the page (`net.onRebase`). With no server (no SDK and no `VITE_SERVER_URL`, or nobody answers) the game plays offline on the middle pen with the `NEIGHBOURS` stand-ins. Local backend: set `VITE_SERVER_URL=ws://localhost:2567` in `.env`.
- Run: the `game` entry in `..\.claude\launch.json` (vite dev, `--mode development`).

## The game
A lego-style hub (sunken stud meadow ringed by two tiers of grass-topped dirt cliffs). Five fenced pens line the south side; the middle one is yours. North of the meadow the yellow road runs east-west; the lane opens north between the leaderboards (west wall) and the Sell stall, Fuse Machine and free chest (east). Loop: step onto the yellow throw band at the lane mouth and the throw sequence takes over (game/throw.js): the camera swings behind you, a power meter sweeps up and down, tap (click / Space / F / E / touch anywhere) to stop it (PERFECT/GREAT/GOOD/WEAK grade multiplies luck); the camera chases the food down the lane (speed lines, wind streaks, the LUCK counter climbs with distance, each rarity zone slams its name), slow-mo + hit-stop touchdown, bounces and a short orbit, then (no prompt) a first-person cutscene through the eyes of a baddie of that zone: it rises from behind the nearest bush, spots the food, walks in (head bob, sway, roll, footsteps) and eats it bite by bite; a flash cuts to a close-up of her and her name types out before the rarity card ($/s, NEW!, PEN FULL!); continue and she is caught into the **hotbar** (spins into sparkles, her card flies into a slot) or walks off if there is no room, and a wipe returns you to the meter. Back in the hub, select a hotbar slot (click / number key) to carry her in your arms; a white disc shows where she goes, and inside your pen click (E / F, touch PLACE) to place her there; only placed baddies earn. Exit (Esc / Q) leaves; Auto plays the loop; the Lucky Throw (100x luck for one throw) costs money, priced from your income. Baddies earn $/s. Training at the Training Bench (pull-ups) earns XP; training levels give strength. **Throw range** = the food's base distance (`dist`, 20 m x1.08 a tier) x (1 + strength x `THROW.strengthPct`, capped at +`strengthCap`), so both buying food and training matter; Iron Arms boosts training XP (not strength). **Pacing**: the food prices (per tier in `FOOD_LIST`), `TRAINING.need` / `benches`, `AREA_INCOME` and `THROW` were tuned together in a progression sim so a player who throws and trains reaches the last area in about 2 hours (no area gates); change them together. Money buys foods (49 tiers Common .. Exotic plus the Galaxy Donut, `FOODS` / `FOOD_RARITIES` in config; each tier adds range and luck; the rarer the food, the bigger its aura, ribbon trail, landing show and sounds), pen capacity, Shop upgrades. 3 of a kind fuse into BIG (x4), 3 BIG into HUGE (x20). **Nothing costs premium currency** (see the root CLAUDE.md). The lane is a long empty sunken canyon (its floor is `HUB.lane.floorY` = 10 m below the hub, with a sheer dirt drop under the throw band and dirt skirts under the walls; two cliff tiers above, rim trees, wheat and lavender on top, bushes/trees/rocks along the floor edges) split into twelve themed **areas** (`AREAS` in config, mirrored as z ranges in `HUB.zones`): Sunny Meadow, Harvest Farm, Willow Lake, Desert Canyon, Frost Peaks, Autumn Grove, Crystal Falls, Sunset Beach, Coral Reef, Cloud Heaven, Lava Inferno, Cosmic Rift. Each has its floor colours, its own wall props (`cover`: meadow trees, hay and corn, willows and reeds, cacti and mesas, snowy pines and ice, autumn trees and pumpkins, crystals, palms and parasols, coral and kelp, clouds and gold pillars, obsidian and lava pools, alien crystals and meteors), floor accents (lava channels, star specks...), a glowing boundary line + an AREA n name sign on a post by each wall, and the particles that drift through its air (`fx`: petals, wheat, fireflies, sand, snow, leaves, gems, sunset sparks, bubbles, feathers, embers, stars; `ambient()` in main.js spawns them round the camera while it is over the lane). Each area has ten original girls (all made up; never copy existing characters) whose slot sets rarity (`RARITY_SLOTS` + one step every three areas), odds (`SLOT_ODDS`, shown as "1 in N") and income (`AREA_INCOME` x `SLOT_INCOME`). Rarities: Common .. Secret plus Divine, Celestial, Eternal (shimmering cards). Nothing roams it: when food lands, a baddie of that zone steps out from the nearest bush (`laneCover` in hub/world.js) and runs to eat it. The yellow throw band (`buildThrowBand` in hub/props.js, `THROW_MODE.band`) sits just south of the throw-line barrier. Yellow chevrons on the ground lead from the player to the current quest target (`questTarget` in main.js).

## Live events, rewards and collection extras
- **Variants** (`VARIANTS`: GOLD x2 1/20, DIAMOND x5 1/200, RAINBOW x15 1/2000): rolled on every catch in `throw.js rollMeet` (featured area x3, luck helps a little); stored as `v` on pen / hotbar entries; tinted body + ring in `baddieModel.js` (`variant` option, rainbow cycles), stamps on cards / hotbar / reveal card. Fusing needs girl + size + variant to match (`state.fuseKey`). **Sizes** go up to GIANT (x100) and TITAN (x500).
- **Events** (`game/events.js`, all on the UTC clock, no server): Luck Hour (first 10 min of every hour, luck x2), Weekend (Sat/Sun income x2), Featured area (rotates every 30 min: luck x1.5, variants x3), the **Hungry Giant** (`game/boss.js`, at :20 and :50 for 4 min: a giant season girl at the lane end; the room server counts everyone's throws, backend `boss.ts`; offline the client counts its own), and **Seasons** (`SEASONS`, 14 days each, rotating: 4 season girls each, `SEASON_GIRLS`, area -1, entry `a` = the area she was caught in sets her income; 1 in `SEASON.chance` throws; Event Tickets per throw / per season girl; the event shop `EVENT_SHOP` sells potions, Lucky Throws and Season Lures for tickets only). HUD pills over the money (`ui/eventsUi.js`) and the Events window.
- **Rewards window** (`ui/rewards.js`): playtime gifts (`PLAYTIME_GIFTS`, per session), Daily Reward (`DAILY`, server clock via backend `daily.ts`: `claimDaily` -> `dailyResult`), spin wheel (`SPIN`, server rolls via `spin` -> `spinResult`, ids = backend `SPIN_WEDGES`; an unknown id pays the first wedge). Offline both run locally for the session. Prizes go through `state.grantPrize` (cash = secs of income, Lucky Throws, potions `S.potions`, tickets).
- **Throw extras**: PERFECT streak (`STREAK`, +10% luck per PERFECT in a row, GOOD/WEAK resets), distance records + milestone cash (`RECORDS`), `S.stats.bestArea/bestDist/bestStreak/bestVariant/bestRarity`.
- **Quests** (`QUESTS`, ~42): `goal` quests complete themselves (`state.checkQuests`, twice a second); `secs` rewards scale with income (`state.questReward`). **Index rewards** (`INDEX_REWARD`): a finished page pays cash + a title (`S.titles`).
- **Pacing**: all of the above adds money, so food prices are the tuned per-tier list x2.5; the progression sim still puts a throw-and-train player at the last area in about 2 hours. Change rewards and prices together.

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Treat a feature as unfinished until it works on touch and on a small screen.
- **Input**: every action needs a touch path, not just a key. Use Pointer Events (`pointerdown/move/up`, check `e.pointerType`), never mouse-only events. Layout (implemented in `src/touch.js`): left-thumb virtual joystick -> `inputState.move`, right-side drag -> `inputState.look`, on-screen buttons for jump/sprint/mode/etc., pinch -> `inputState.zoom`. Everything still feeds `inputState` in `src/input.js` so game code stays input-agnostic. Pointer lock does not exist on touch; never rely on it.
- **Detection**: decide touch UI with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` / `navigator.maxTouchPoints`, not the user agent. Show touch controls only on coarse pointers; a device can have both.
- **Layout/UI**: HUD and menus use relative units (`%`, `vw/vh`, `dvh`, `clamp()`) and flexbox/grid, no fixed pixel layouts. Tap targets >= 44px. Respect notches with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (viewport already has `viewport-fit=cover`). Text readable at 360px width. No hover-only interactions. Add `touch-action: none` on the canvas and game controls so the browser does not scroll/zoom.
- **Resize/orientation**: anything sized to the screen (camera aspect, renderer, HUD) must react to `resize` (covers orientation changes). Use `visualViewport` where the on-screen keyboard or browser bars matter.
- **Performance**: mobile GPUs are weak. Respect the `graphics_quality` presets in `src/config.js` (cap DPR, shadow map size, draw distance); default to a lower preset on coarse-pointer devices. Avoid per-frame allocations; keep draw calls and texture sizes low.
- **Testing**: check every UI/input change at a phone viewport (e.g. 375x812 and landscape 812x375) with touch emulation, plus desktop, before calling it done.

## Multiplayer presence (net.js, remote.js)
Everything below rides on the server roster (backend `PlayerState`) plus two relayed events; offline play is unchanged.
- `main.js sendPresence`: pose (~10 Hz, only on change + 1 s heartbeat: position, yaw, grounded, vy, right-arm angle, lean, `act` aim/train, equipped food, carried baddie, income), `pen` (placed baddies, 2 Hz signature), `setAvatar` (Bloxity look JSON `{equipped, proportions}`), `throw` (from throw.js `onThrow`: food, from, to, dur, peak), `emote`.
- `remote.js`: one `RemotePlayer` per other player: Bloxity skin / accessories / proportions (`attachEquippedAccessories` + `bloxity.skinTextureUrlFor`), the procedural animator (idle / run / jump), arm poses, the food in hand while aiming, the baddie carried in the arms, name tag, SDK chat bubbles, Bloxity emotes (`avatar/emotes.js`, clips from `GET {apiUrl}/v1/avatar/emotes`) and the replayed throw (`Flight`: same arc, trail, landing burst). Cheap by design: `REMOTE` in config (animator at a third of the rate beyond `farDist`, nothing drawn beyond `hideDist`, particles only within `fxDist`).
- **One unit per player**: base + Food Shop + Training Place. `placeUnit(base)` (config.js) shifts `POI.foodShop` / `TRAINING.spot` beside the assigned base before `main.js` builds them; only your own shop and training station exist (other bases show just the pen + occupant). After a girl lands in the hotbar the throw ends and `throw.js leaveToBase()` respawns you at your base (Auto mode still loops back to the meter).
- `Pen.syncRemote(str, income)`: another player's base shows their placed baddies (the existing wandering / appeal animations and particles), income and name; `Pen.setOccupant` the name + picture or "Free base".
- Bloxity SDK (`bloxity.js`): `registerFeature('emotes' | 'play_with_friends')`, `party.enable({ maxPartySize: bases })` + `party.joined`, `game.updateRoom` (`relay~room`), `?roomId=` launch join (`net.js joinLaunchRoom`), `playerJoined / playerInRoom`, chat bubbles, emote events, ad breaks (pause + mute; screens declared but off, no rewarded ads), leaderboards (`LEADERBOARDS`: submit every 30 s, top lists replace `BOARD_BOTS` when the SDK answers), settings `master_volume` / `music_volume`, friend boost = Bloxity friends in your room (`state.setFriends`), tap the FRIEND BOOST badge to copy an invite link. No gems / purchases (see the root CLAUDE.md).
- Dev: `launch.json` entry `net-test` (vite on 5252 with `VITE_SERVER_URL=ws://localhost:2567`) + the backend (`npm start` there). Browser tabs in the background do not run the render loop, so they stop sending poses: front the tab you are testing. `__game` exposes `net` and `remote`.

## Progress is saved on the server only
- `game/state.js` has no browser storage: `hydrate(doc)` loads the server's document at boot (`net.loadGame()`, waits for the backend's `game` message; `serverError` / timeout = play without saving so a save that could not be read is never overwritten) and `save()` (debounced `ECONOMY.saveEveryMs`, plus on tab hide / unload) sends the whole `S` through `net.saveGame` -> backend `saveGame` (bounded by `game.ts sanitizeGame`, stored as the `game` field of the player's Mongo doc). The Index "NEW" tags live in `S.seen`. Offline, as a guest (never stored) or with no database the session just is not stored. Only device preferences stay in the browser (sound volume / mute, the Active panel fold).
- A new field in `S`: add it to `fresh()` AND to backend `GameSave` / `sanitizeGame`, or the server drops it.
- Rewarded ads (`ads.js`, `ADS` in config): Free Gift button (only while `bloxity.adsAvailable()`; simulated in `npm run dev` without the SDK) -> `bloxity.showRewarded(placement)` -> only on 'completed' the server verifies the token (`net.verifyAd` -> backend `adReward` -> Bloxity `/v1/ads/reward/verify`) -> reward (2x income for 10 min = `S.adBoostUntil`, a cash boost, a free Lucky Throw = `S.luckyCredits`). Wins / money only. Gameplay banners: `bloxity.setAdScreen('gameplay' | 'panel' | null)` each 0.5 s (none during throws, training, ad breaks), declared with `declareScreens({ gameplay: { show: true } })`; only the two right-hand corners are left free for banners.

## Rooms and leaderboards on Bloxity
- Open Rooms needs the backend's room reporter (see its CLAUDE.md) plus the client's `updateRoom` (`net.js reportRoom`, `relay~room`) and `?roomId=` join (`net.js joinLaunchRoom` -> `bloxity.locateRoom`).
- The client sends its Bloxity token (`bloxity.getToken()`) on join and `identify`: on a deployed server it is the only way to be a signed-in player (progress saved, leaderboards).
- Plaza boards: the server's `leaderboard` message (`net.onLeaderboard`, `LEADERBOARDS[i].stat`) first, then Bloxity's top list, then the offline stand-ins. Bloxity boards: `LEADERBOARDS` in config (same ids / settings as the backend; permanent once declared).
- `BLOXITY_CODE_METADATA.md` answers Bloxity's SDK-integration audit questions: keep it current.

## Ads policy and the friend invite boost
